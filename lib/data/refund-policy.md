# Phonemarket Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026  
**Applies to:** Orders placed through Phonemarket's company-assisted checkout. Purchases arranged directly between a buyer and an independent seller may be subject to the seller's own terms.

## Our buying options

Phonemarket supports two transaction routes:

- **Buy directly:** The buyer coordinates payment, collection, delivery, and any dispute with the seller. Phonemarket's platform rules and applicable law still apply, but Phonemarket does not automatically hold or inspect the device.
- **Buy through Phonemarket:** The seller sends the device to Phonemarket for an inspection before it is forwarded to the buyer. The order record states the inspection outcome and any applicable satisfaction or return terms.

Always check the specific listing and order confirmation to see which route you selected.

## Cancellation before dispatch

A buyer may request cancellation while an order is awaiting seller handover or inspection. Approval depends on the order's current status and whether non-recoverable costs have already been incurred. Contact support with the order number as soon as possible.

Once a device has been dispatched to the buyer, the request is handled as a return request rather than a pre-dispatch cancellation.

## Returns for company-assisted purchases

For an eligible purchase made through Phonemarket, contact support within **7 calendar days of confirmed delivery** to report a material mismatch, an undisclosed functional fault, or another issue covered by the order's stated satisfaction terms. Keep the device, packaging, accessories, and proof of delivery until the case is resolved.

A return is not automatically approved for a change of mind, accidental damage after delivery, liquid damage, unauthorized repair, or damage caused by misuse. The device may be inspected to determine whether the reported issue matches the order record.

## Incorrect, damaged, or materially misdescribed devices

Report the issue promptly and include the order number, a description of the problem, and clear photos or video where relevant. Do not attempt a repair before support responds. If the claim is approved, Phonemarket will explain the available remedy, which may include a return, replacement where available, or refund.

## Refund processing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where technically possible. If that is not possible, support will agree on an appropriate alternative with the buyer. Processing time depends on the payment provider and bank; allow **5–10 business days after refund approval** for the funds to appear. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Cash-on-delivery orders, where offered, may require additional verification before a refund can be completed.

## Return condition and inspection

Returned phones must include the items supplied with the order. Do not remove or alter device identifiers, security seals, or inspection labels. Phonemarket may compare the returned device's IMEI/serial number and condition against the original inspection record.

## How to request help

Contact **support@phonemarket.pk** and include:

- Order number
- Name used on the order
- Delivery date
- Description of the issue
- Supporting photos or video, if available
