# Phonemarket Store Information

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

## Business overview

Phonemarket is an online marketplace focused on buying and selling mobile phones in Pakistan. It supports individual sellers and registered businesses. Eligible company-assisted orders may be routed through Phonemarket for inspection before delivery.

## Contact

- **Customer support:** support@phonemarket.pk
- **Website:** https://phonemarket.pk (placeholder; confirm the production domain before publication)
- **Support hours:** Monday–Saturday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time (PKT, UTC+5), excluding public holidays.
- **Expected response time:** Within 1 business day for routine enquiries. Complex order, inspection, or payment cases may take longer.

These contact details and hours are proposed defaults and must be verified before going live.

## Locations and visits

Phonemarket operates as an online-first marketplace. No public walk-in store or inspection-center address is confirmed in this knowledge base. Do not tell a customer to visit a physical location unless an approved address and appointment instructions are available in the current system.

For a company-assisted order, use the pickup, shipping, or inspection instructions supplied with that order. Sellers should not send devices to an unconfirmed address.

## Service area

The proposed initial service area is Pakistan, subject to courier coverage and the delivery address. Serviceability must be confirmed during checkout. Availability of company-assisted inspection may vary by location.

## Seller types

- **Individual seller:** A customer listing a personally owned phone.
- **Business seller:** A shop or registered business listing phones.
- **Verified seller:** A seller whose submitted information has passed the platform's verification review. Verification is not a blanket guarantee of product quality.
- **Company-assisted transaction:** A purchase route in which Phonemarket receives a device for inspection before forwarding it to the buyer.

## Safety and privacy

Use the platform's secure flows for identity verification, payments, and order details. Do not send passwords or one-time passwords to support. Share CNIC details or a full IMEI only when necessary and through an approved secure channel.

## RAG guidance

If asked for a physical address, do not invent one. State that no public walk-in address is currently confirmed and direct the customer to support for the relevant order or appointment. If asked about current support availability, use the published schedule only after the business has verified it.
