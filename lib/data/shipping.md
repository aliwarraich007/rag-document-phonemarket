# Phonemarket Shipping and Delivery

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026  
**Coverage:** Pakistan (proposed service policy; availability depends on the listing and delivery address).

## Delivery methods

Delivery options are shown during checkout or agreed between the buyer and seller.

- **Company-assisted delivery:** The seller sends the phone to Phonemarket for inspection. After the device passes the required checks and the order is cleared for dispatch, Phonemarket arranges delivery to the buyer.
- **Direct seller delivery or collection:** The buyer and seller coordinate the handover directly. Delivery timing, fees, and handover arrangements must be confirmed with the seller.

Not every listing supports both options. The order confirmation is the source of truth for the selected route.

## Estimated delivery times

For company-assisted orders, use these planning estimates after the phone reaches the inspection stage:

- Major urban areas: **2–5 business days** after inspection clearance.
- Other serviceable areas: **4–8 business days** after inspection clearance.

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. Seller handover delays, inspection findings, public holidays, weather, courier disruptions, and incomplete addresses may extend delivery.

## Shipping fees

The applicable delivery fee is displayed at checkout or confirmed before the order is finalized. Fees may vary by destination, package size, courier, and delivery method. Do not assume shipping is free unless the checkout explicitly says so.

## Tracking and delivery updates

Where tracking is available, Phonemarket or its delivery partner will share the tracking reference through the contact details on the order. Check the order page first. If tracking has not updated for two business days, contact support with the order number.

## Delivery address and recipient

Buyers are responsible for providing a complete address and reachable phone number. If an address or phone number needs to change, contact support before dispatch. Changes may not be possible once the parcel is with the courier.

The recipient may be asked to confirm their identity or provide an order reference at handover. Do not share one-time passwords or account credentials with a courier.

## Failed delivery or damaged package

If delivery fails, the courier may attempt redelivery or return the parcel according to its procedures. Additional delivery attempts or return charges may apply if the failure results from an incorrect address or an unavailable recipient.

If a package appears damaged, photograph the packaging before opening it where practical, then photograph the device and report the issue promptly. Keep all packaging until support advises otherwise.

## Shipping a phone to Phonemarket for inspection

When a seller uses company-assisted checkout, follow the handover instructions provided for that order. Package the device securely, disclose known faults, and include only the accessories specified in the listing or handover instructions. Do not ship a device until the order's handover instructions are confirmed.

## Support

For delivery questions, email **support@phonemarket.pk** with the order number, destination city, and tracking reference (if available).
