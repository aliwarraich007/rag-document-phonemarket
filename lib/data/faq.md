# Phonemarket Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Phonemarket?

Phonemarket is a mobile-phone marketplace designed to connect buyers with individual sellers and businesses. It aims to make listings easier to compare and transactions more trustworthy through seller verification and optional company-assisted inspection.

## Who can sell on Phonemarket?

Both individual customers and registered businesses can list phones, subject to platform rules and account checks. Sellers should provide accurate device details, disclose known faults, and use genuine photos.

## What does a verified seller badge mean?

A verified badge means the seller has completed the platform's applicable verification process. The process may involve reviewing business proof and CNIC information. A badge is not a guarantee that every listing is genuine, that a device is fault-free, or that a transaction is risk-free.

## What is the difference between buying directly and buying through Phonemarket?

With **Buy directly**, the buyer coordinates the transaction and handover with the seller. With **Buy through Phonemarket**, the phone is sent to Phonemarket for inspection before being delivered to the buyer under the order's terms. Availability and fees are shown for the individual listing or checkout.

## Does Phonemarket inspect every phone?

No. Inspection applies to eligible orders that use the company-assisted route. Direct purchases should not be assumed to include a Phonemarket inspection.

## Does an inspection guarantee the phone is perfect?

No. An inspection checks the phone's condition and functions at a particular time. Some hidden or intermittent problems may not be detected. Review the inspection result and disclosed condition before accepting the order.

## Are all phones PTA approved?

No. PTA status varies by device and listing. Check the listing's stated PTA status and verify the device through the relevant official channels before buying. Do not assume that a phone is approved simply because it is listed on Phonemarket.

## How do I know a phone's battery health?

Battery health depends on the model and the information available from the device. Where supported, the seller should state the reported battery health and provide evidence on request. Battery health may not be available or directly comparable on every Android model.

## Can I return a phone?

Return eligibility depends on the transaction route and order terms. For eligible company-assisted purchases, the proposed policy asks buyers to report covered issues within 7 calendar days of confirmed delivery. Direct purchases are handled under the seller's terms and applicable law. Check the current policy and order confirmation.

## How long does delivery take?

Proposed estimates for company-assisted delivery are 2–5 business days after inspection clearance for major urban areas and 4–8 business days for other serviceable areas. These are estimates, and the order confirmation is authoritative.

## How can I become a verified seller?

Create a seller account and follow the verification flow. The platform may request business proof and CNIC information for review. Submit only accurate information through the secure platform flow. Approval is subject to review.

## What if a listing contains incorrect information?

Save the listing URL or ID and contact support with the details that appear incorrect. Include screenshots if useful. Do not post another person's private information publicly.

## Can I feature or boost my listing?

The platform is designed to support featured ads and listing boosts. Available packages, pricing, eligibility, and any verified-seller discounts should be checked in the seller dashboard; do not assume a discount applies unless it is displayed there.

## How do I contact support?

Email **support@phonemarket.pk**. Include the order number or listing ID, a concise description of the issue, and the best way to reach you. Never email passwords, one-time passwords, or unnecessary identity documents.

## Is Phonemarket a phone manufacturer?

No. Phonemarket is a marketplace and transaction facilitator. A phone may be listed by an individual, business, brand, or other eligible seller; the listing identifies the seller.
