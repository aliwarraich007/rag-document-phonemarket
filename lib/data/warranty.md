# Phonemarket Warranty and Device Condition

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

## 1. Marketplace devices and warranty coverage

Phonemarket lists phones from individual sellers, business sellers, and potentially registered brands or the company itself. Warranty coverage depends on the seller, device, listing, and order terms. A listing must not be described as having a manufacturer or seller warranty unless that coverage has been verified and its terms are available.

**A used phone does not automatically include a Phonemarket warranty.** Company-assisted inspection is a condition check at a point in time; it is not, by itself, a promise that every component will remain fault-free for a specified period.

## 2. Manufacturer warranty

If a phone is advertised with an active manufacturer warranty, the buyer should confirm the warranty period, regional eligibility, proof-of-purchase requirements, and whether the warranty is valid in Pakistan. Manufacturer warranty decisions are made by the relevant manufacturer or authorized service provider.

## 3. Seller warranty

A business seller may offer its own warranty. The listing or order confirmation should specify:
- Warranty duration and start date
- Covered parts and faults
- Exclusions, including accidental or liquid damage where applicable
- Claim process and required proof
- Whether repair, replacement, or another remedy is available

If those details are not stated, ask the seller before buying rather than assuming coverage.

## 4. Inspection for company-assisted purchases

For a company-assisted order, Phonemarket receives the device and performs an inspection before delivery. The inspection may cover visible condition, core functions, device identifiers, and the details disclosed in the listing. The precise checklist and outcome should be recorded against the order.

Inspection reduces uncertainty but cannot guarantee that every intermittent, hidden, or future fault will be detected. Buyers should read the inspection report and the listing condition carefully.

## 5. Reporting a fault

If a device develops a problem, review the order's warranty and return terms first. Contact **support@phonemarket.pk** with:
- Order number and device model
- IMEI or serial number, shared only through the support channel when needed
- Description of the fault and when it started
- Photos, video, or diagnostic information where useful
- Any inspection report or seller warranty document

Do not publish a full IMEI or other device identifiers in a public listing or community post.

## 6. Common exclusions

Unless the specific written warranty says otherwise, coverage generally does not include damage caused by drops, liquid, misuse, unauthorized opening or repair, tampering, or normal cosmetic wear. A seller's written terms and applicable law take precedence over this general summary.

## 7. Who handles the claim?

- **Direct purchase:** Start with the seller and the warranty terms attached to the listing or receipt.
- **Company-assisted purchase:** Contact Phonemarket support, which will review the order and route the claim under the applicable return or warranty terms.
- **Manufacturer warranty:** Contact the manufacturer or authorized service provider where required.
